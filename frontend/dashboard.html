Self-Healing

Validated via a controlled fixture page (github.com/saad0O5/ReTrace-Fixture): forced a real structural break, confirmed extraction failed cleanly, then triggered Bright Data's Self-Healing. The dashboard correctly diagnosed the change and generated accurate new selectors, verified against a live preview with real extracted data. The final publish step hit a transient platform error ("Could not connect to preview server") during our testing window — the fix-generation mechanism itself is confirmed working end to end.
